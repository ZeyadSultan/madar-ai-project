07Assumptions and open questions

  1. 01The kit has no error red. Form errors and the failed-answer alert use the warning colours.
  2. 02Palette 1c: indigo surfaces, teal-light (#8FE0CF) accents and citations, terracotta (#E27A4E) for all buttons with white text (≈3.2:1 — review for AA). Light mode uses deep teal buttons for contrast.
  3. 03"Start asking" goes to sign-up. Picking a subject on the landing page carries through sign-up straight into that subject's chat.
  4. 04A new chat is only listed in history after its first question. Chats are titled by their first question.
  5. 05One citation opens at a time in the source panel. Tapping the other citation line swaps it; tapping the open one closes it.
  6. 06Out-of-syllabus answers end with a muted "Not in the syllabus" line where the citation would be, so every answer ends the same way.
  7. 07Settings holds only language and account (name, email, sign out). Password reset is a link on sign-in and isn't designed here.
  8. 08Tablet (700–1099px) hides the sidebar behind a menu button and opens the source panel as an overlay. Desktop docks both.
  9. 09English only for now; Arabic RTL screens are parked (the prototype keeps a language switch). Syllabus topics, page numbers and excerpts are fictional placeholders. In the prototype, any question that doesn't match the sample content gets the out-of-syllabus reply.
  10. 10The Madar mark replaces the indigo Madar AI mark. The thinking animation reuses the orbit-ring motion, recoloured teal with a terracotta core. Confirm with the brand owner.
  11. 11Avatars show the first initial only, because sign-up asks for a first name alone. If there's no name, the email's first letter is used.
